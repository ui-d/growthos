## Why it matters

Random check-ins = random results. A disciplined cadence creates accountability, catches problems early, and builds a culture of continuous improvement.

## Template

**Growth Cadence Stack:**
```
Daily:
- Automated alerts (5 min)
- Key metrics check (10 min)

Weekly:
- Experiment reviews (30 min)
- Metrics deep-dive (1 hr)
- Growth team sync (45 min)

Monthly:
- Board review (2 hrs)
- Quarterly planning (4 hrs)
- Post-mortems (1 hr)
```

**Meeting Format:**
1. Metrics review (what happened?)
2. Root cause analysis (why?)
3. Action items (what next?)
4. Owner + deadline (who + when?)

## Common mistakes

1. **Meetings without data** - Opinions dominate facts
2. **No follow-up** - Same discussions every week
3. **Wrong frequency** - Daily reviews of monthly metrics
4. **Too many attendees** - Decision paralysis

## Example output

**Amazon's Weekly Business Review:**
```
Format: 6-page narrative memo
Duration: 60 minutes sharp

Agenda:
0-20min: Silent reading of memo
20-50min: Deep dive on exceptions
50-60min: Decisions and actions

Rules:
- No PowerPoints
- Data > Anecdotes
- Written commitments
- Public scorecard

Output Example:
"Prime cancellations up 3% in Germany
Root cause: Delivery delays from new FC
Action: Reroute 30% volume to FC-Berlin
Owner: Hans M.
Deadline: EOD Thursday"
```

Result: 10x faster decision velocity.
