## Why it matters
The most expensive growth is growth built on bad data. Governance keeps your tracking spec enforceable and your dashboards trustworthy.

## Template
### Monthly audit (15–30 min)
- Core event coverage (did anything drop?)
- Duplicate events/properties
- ID integrity (`user_id`, `workspace_id`)
- Environment consistency

### Alerts (minimum)
- sudden drop in `signup_completed`
- sudden drop in `workspace_created`
- sudden drop in `activation_completed`
- spikes in null/unknown for key properties

### Implementation rules
- Critical events: prefer server-side when feasible
- New event requires: spec update + review

## Common mistakes
- No environment separation.
- Hotfix tracking without updating the spec.
- A tracking plan that exists but isn't enforced.

## Example output
Audit checklist + 4 alerts + a simple "new event requires review" process.
