## Why it matters
Activation is not "logged in." It's the **first measurable moment of value** that correlates with retention. A strong definition makes onboarding measurable and turns "improve activation" into an engineering-ready spec.

## Template
### Activation definition
- **Activation event:** `activation_completed`
- **Window:** 24h from `signup_completed` (use 7d for longer setups)
- **Rules (workspace-level):**
  1) `workspace_created`
  2) `primary_object_created` (project/app/dashboard)
  3) `key_feature_used(feature="X")` **or** `integration_connected`

### Metrics
- **Activation Rate:** activated_workspaces / signup_completed_workspaces
- **TTV:** median(signup_completed → activation_completed)

### Funnel breakdown
- Step completion: onboarding step conversion + time per step

## Common mistakes
- Too easy (doesn't represent value).
- Too hard (too many steps → drop-off).
- No segmentation (source/plan/role) → you can't diagnose what works.

## Example output (Devtool)
Activation = workspace created + app created + first deploy/publish **or** integration connected + teammate invited.
