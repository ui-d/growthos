## Why it matters

Most users quit before experiencing value. A clear activation metric identifies who "gets it" so you can optimize the journey for everyone else.

## Template

**Activation Formula:**
```
Activated User =
  Performed [key action(s)]
  Within [time period]
  [frequency] times
```

**Research Process:**
1. Analyze cohort retention curves
2. Identify inflection point behaviors
3. Validate with user interviews
4. Test predictive power

## Common mistakes

1. **Too complex** - "Do 5 things in 3 different features"
2. **Too simple** - "Logged in once"
3. **Wrong timing** - Measuring at 30 days when churn happens at day 7
4. **Feature-focused** - "Used feature X" vs "achieved outcome Y"

## Example output

**Slack's Activation:**
- **Metric**: Team sends 2,000+ messages
- **Timing**: Within first 30 days
- **Why it works**: Shows team adoption, not just individual usage
- **Result**: 93% of activated teams convert to paid

**Facebook's 7 Friends in 10 Days:**
- Simple to understand
- Easy to influence through product
- Strong correlation with long-term retention
- Became north star for growth team
